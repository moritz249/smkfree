Privacy / Legal

Private non-commercial project

Purpose

SMKFREE is a private, non-commercial smoke-free progress tracker. It creates a receipt-style summary from the details you enter. The app is for motivation only.

No medical or financial advice

Health benefits, estimated life back, cigarette counts, and cost totals are rough motivational estimates. They are not medical, financial, or professional advice. Actual effects and costs vary from person to person.

Health estimate sources

Tobacco milestones and the Health tab follow the timeline of the German Cancer Research Center, DKFZ: Rauchstopp (see also the CDC benefits of quitting timeline). The percentages in the Health tab show the share of time passed until each point in time the source names (for a range, its end). They are a simple estimate for motivation, not a measurement of body functions. The quit line number (BIÖG, 0800 8 31 31 31) is taken from the same DKFZ page. The cigarette life-expectancy figure uses a population-level estimate of 20 minutes per cigarette from Jackson, Jarvis, and West, The price of a cigarette: 20 minutes of life? (Addiction, 2025). This estimate is based on population averages and is not a prediction of an individual person's lifespan. It does not apply to vaping or roll-ups.

Local storage

Your entries, savings goal and settings are saved only on your device: in the browser's localStorage on the website, and additionally in the app's local storage on iPhone and Android. Nothing is sent to a server. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. SMKFREE does not run user accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Use Reset inside the app, or delete the app, to remove the locally saved data.
